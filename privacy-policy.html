CoverNet Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026

The short version: CoverNet connects people with licensed financial professionals. We collect only what is needed to do that. Your private details (age, date of birth, height, health notes) are hidden from agents unless you choose to share them for a specific request, and sharing stops when the request ends. We never sell your information.

Who we are

CoverNet is published by Zain Adtani. Questions or requests: za.backup1@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account: your name, email address and password (the password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication, and we never see it).
  • When you request an appointment: your name, email, optional phone number, how you prefer to be contacted, the service you want, the time you pick, and an optional note.
  • Optional private details: age, date of birth, height and health notes. These are optional, off by default, and shared with an agent only if you turn sharing on for that request.
  • Messages you send to an agent or receive from one.
  • For agents: profile details (name, bio, location, license information, services), availability, and subscription status.

Please do not enter Social Security numbers, bank or card numbers, or other highly sensitive identifiers in the app.

Who can see your information

  • Only the agent you send a request to can see your contact details, and only while the request is open (requested or confirmed).
  • Your private details are visible to that agent only if you chose to share them. If the request is declined, cancelled, or the connection ends, sharing turns off and your contact details are hidden from the agent again.
  • Other clients and other agents cannot see your requests or messages.
  • The app owner may review agent applications and see summary counts (such as how many agents are active). The owner does not routinely read private messages.

How we protect it

Your data is stored with Google Firebase (Google Cloud). Google encrypts data in transit and at rest. The app cannot read the database directly: every request goes through server code that checks who you are and what you are allowed to see. We test these rules, but no system is 100% secure, so please share only what you are comfortable sharing.

What we do not do

We do not sell your information, show ads, or use third-party advertising or analytics tools in the app. CoverNet connects people with agents. It does not give financial advice, and agents are responsible for the advice they give.

Payments

Clients pay nothing. Agent memberships are billed through Apple. We do not see or store card numbers.

Keeping and deleting your information

You can delete your account inside the app (Account, then Delete account). This permanently deletes your profile, requests, messages and, for agents, your agent profile and follow-ups. If you have a paid agent membership, cancel it in your Apple subscription settings first, because deleting an account does not cancel billing.

Children

CoverNet is for adults. It is not meant for children under 18.

Apple and TestFlight

If you install through Apple's TestFlight or the App Store, Apple may collect information under Apple's own privacy policy, such as crash reports or feedback you choose to send.

Changes

If we change what we collect or how we use it, we will update this page and the date above.

This policy is a plain-language draft and should be reviewed by a lawyer before public launch.
