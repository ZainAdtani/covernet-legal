Common questions

Is PathFolk free?

Yes for clients. Licensed agents need an active membership to be listed and to use agent tools. We will share membership details at launch.

Who can see my private details?

Your private details are your age, date of birth and height. They are optional. Only the agent you send a request to can see them, and only if you turn on sharing for that request. You can turn sharing off any time in the Appointments tab. Declining, cancelling, or ending a connection also turns sharing off and hides your contact details from that agent. See our Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my account?

Open the Account tab and tap Delete my account. This permanently deletes your account, requests and messages. Our Privacy Policy explains the few records we keep. If you are an agent with a paid membership, cancel the membership first. Deleting your account does not cancel billing.

Does PathFolk give financial advice?

No. PathFolk helps you find licensed professionals and request an appointment. It does not give insurance, tax, legal or investment advice. The agent you choose is responsible for the advice they give.

I am an agent. How do I get listed?

Create an account, open the Account tab, tap Apply as an agent, and fill in your profile and license details. We check your license against public state records before your profile appears. You also need an active membership. We will share membership details at launch.
