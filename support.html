Common questions

Is CoverNet free?

Yes for clients. Licensed agents pay a monthly membership to be listed and use agent tools.

Who can see my private details?

Only the agent you send a request to, and only if you turn on sharing for that request. Declining, cancelling, or ending a connection turns sharing off. See our Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my account?

Open the Account tab and tap Delete account. If you are an agent with a paid plan, cancel it in your Apple subscription settings first.

Does CoverNet give financial advice?

No. CoverNet connects you with licensed professionals. They are responsible for the advice they give.

I am an agent. How do I get listed?

Create an account, tap Apply as an agent, and fill in your profile. Profiles are reviewed before they appear, and an active membership is required.
